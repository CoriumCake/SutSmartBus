SUT Smart Bus Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the SUT Smart Bus application and related services collect, use, store, and protect information for campus bus tracking, route information, ride assistance, feedback, and environmental monitoring features.

Who operates this service

SUT Smart Bus is operated for the Suranaree University of Technology community by the SUT Smart Bus project administrators and responsible university support team.

Information we collect or process

The app may process your device location when you grant location permission. Location is used to show your position on the map, find nearby buses and stops, start or verify an active ride session, and support the bus bell feature while you are near a bus.

When you use ride-related features, the service may receive an app-generated installation identifier, your selected bus, your current location, session status, and timestamps. This helps verify that the feature is used near the relevant bus and helps prevent misuse.

If you submit feedback, we collect the message you provide and the optional name you enter. If no name is entered, the app submits a generic app user label.

The app stores preferences such as theme, language, notification setting, and legal consent locally on your device. The service also displays bus and sensor telemetry, including bus GPS positions, passenger counts, Wi-Fi readings, and air quality measurements. That telemetry belongs to the transport and sensor system, not to app users.

How we use information

We use information to operate app features, display buses and routes, support ride and bell workflows, respond to feedback, troubleshoot service issues, maintain security, and improve campus transit operations.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be handled by authorized university staff, project maintainers, service operators, hosting providers, or infrastructure providers only as needed to operate, secure, maintain, review, or improve the SUT Smart Bus service.

Map tiles and network services may receive standard technical request data, such as IP address and requested map tile coordinates, when the app loads map content.

Security

Production app traffic is designed to use encrypted HTTPS and secure WebSocket connections. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no internet-connected system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Retention and deletion

We keep information only for as long as reasonably needed for operation, troubleshooting, safety, analytics, research, or compliance. Ride session and feedback retention may vary depending on operational needs. To ask about privacy or deletion, contact the responsible SUT Smart Bus support channel.

Your choices

You can deny or revoke location permission in Android settings. Some app features may not work without location. You can also manage local app preferences such as theme, language, and notifications from the app settings.

Children

SUT Smart Bus is intended for the university transport community and is not designed for children to independently submit personal information.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy when the service, legal requirements, or data practices change. The effective date above shows the latest version.

Contact

Privacy questions or requests should be directed to the SUT Smart Bus project administrators or the responsible university support channel. Current support contact: support@sut.ac.th.
